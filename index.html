Simple Process

How the System Works

1. Register

Select your appropriate role (Donor, Hospital, or Organization) and submit your profile.

2. Account Approval

Administrators review and securely approve valid accounts to ensure platform integrity.

3. Access Dashboard

Log in to access your role-specific tools, data, and active workflows.

4. Coordinate

Donors view requests, hospitals submit needs, and organizations process inventory.

Enterprise-Grade Security

Your medical data and user privacy are protected by industry-standard Firebase Authentication and robust, role-based Firestore security rules. Data segregation ensures that users only access what they are strictly authorized to see.
