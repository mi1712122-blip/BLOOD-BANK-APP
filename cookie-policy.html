1. Cookies and similar storage

Cookies are small files a website can store in your browser. Web applications may also use browser storage, such as local storage, to remember settings or support application functions.

2. How the platform uses them

Cookies or browser storage may be used to support authentication and session continuity, help protect accounts, and remember preferences such as the selected display theme. The exact storage used can depend on the Firebase and browser configuration.

3. Types of storage

Authentication and essential operation

Firebase Authentication may use browser-managed storage or cookies to keep a user signed in and support account security. Blocking this storage may prevent sign-in or interrupt protected features.

Preferences

The application may use local storage for settings such as light or dark theme. These settings remain in your browser until cleared or changed.

Analytics and advertising

This project does not intentionally use its landing page to place advertising cookies. If analytics or additional third-party tools are added later, this policy should be updated to describe them.

4. Your controls

Most browsers let you review, block, or delete cookies and site data in their settings. Clearing browser storage may sign you out or reset saved preferences. If you block essential storage, some platform features may not work as expected.

5. Policy updates

We may update this page if storage use or application features change. The current version and update date will appear here.

6. Contact

For questions about cookies or browser storage, contact the system administrator through the home page contact form.

Your browser’s controls affect storage on your device. They do not delete account or workflow records stored by the application.
