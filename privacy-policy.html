1. Information we handle

The information entered into the application depends on the account role and features used. It may include:

  • Account details such as name, email address, and authentication identifiers.
  • Profile and contact details such as phone number, city or location, and optional profile image.
  • Blood group and donation-related details supplied by donors.
  • Hospital requests, inventory records, donation records, and related workflow information supplied by participating users.
  • Messages submitted through the contact form.

Please provide only information needed for the application and do not submit unrelated sensitive details.

2. How information is used

Application information is used to create and maintain accounts, support role-specific dashboards, coordinate donation and request workflows, display relevant records to authorized users, respond to support messages, and maintain the operation and security of the service.

3. Storage and service providers

The application is built using Firebase services. Depending on configured features, account, application, and uploaded file data may be processed through Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, or Firebase Storage. These services process data under their own terms and privacy documentation.

4. Sharing and access

The application is designed to make relevant workflow information available to users with the appropriate role and permissions. For example, information associated with a blood request may be visible to the organization handling that request.

We do not claim that information is never shared: data may be processed by the hosting and infrastructure providers used to operate the application, and may be disclosed where required by applicable law. The project does not sell personal information.

5. Security and retention

Authentication and database access rules are used to help control access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information may remain in application records or backups for as long as needed to operate the service, meet project requirements, or address security and operational issues.

6. Your choices

Keep your account information accurate and protect your login credentials. To request correction or removal of account information, contact the system administrator. Some records may need to remain where they are connected to platform operations or other users’ workflows.

7. Cookies and browser storage

The application may use browser storage or authentication-related cookies to maintain sessions and remember preferences, such as the selected theme. See the Cookie Policy for more detail.

8. Policy updates

This policy may change as the application changes. The latest version and its update date will be posted on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the system administrator through the application or the contact form on the home page.

This page describes the current project at a general level and is not a substitute for legal advice or a complete notice for a production healthcare service.
